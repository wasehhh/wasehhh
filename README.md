## Waseh Niazi

Behaviour therapist working in autism services, partway through a Master of Data Science at the University of Pittsburgh. Most of what I build sits where clinical work and data meet, mainly because that is where I actually spend my time.

ABA generates a lot of data. A therapist records something every few minutes of every session, for years. Almost all of it gets reviewed one client at a time, one graph at a time. I am interested in what it looks like from the other direction.

**Here**

- **[aba_behaviour_analysis](https://github.com/wasehhh/aba_behaviour_analysis)**, a connected series of analyses on ABA session data. Descriptive exploration, then multivariate interaction effects, then a classification model. Synthetic data throughout, documented as such.
